预算与总体性能 对应 Figure 2
With 145 MLS training profiles (1% budget), Transfer reduced pooled 2022–2024 RMSE from 4.709 K for Scratch to 4.359 K, a 7.45% reduction (Figure 2a). The corresponding reductions were 1.03% and 0.80% at the 10% and 100% budgets. Transfer also had lower pooled RMSE than the training-only pressure-offset ERA5 reference at all three budgets (Figure 2b). The paired band comparisons show that the pretraining effect varies across the vertical coordinate, with a larger and more consistent advantage at the smallest training budget (Figures 2c–2e).

时间与垂直结构 对应 Figure 3
Monthly pressure sections resolve temporal and vertical variation that is compressed in pooled scores (Figure 3). At the 1% budget, negative Transfer–Scratch contrasts are widespread, whereas the smaller high-budget gains coexist with localized positive contrasts. The aligned profiles summarize scores pooled within 2022–2024 and within 2025; they are not averages of monthly RMSEs. These descriptive patterns identify where and when pretraining helps without assigning significance to individual months or pressure nodes.

年度与 Hunga 窗口 对应 Figure 4
Four of nine annual year-budget comparisons and eight of nine retrospective Hunga-window comparisons had adjusted 14-day-block intervals wholly below zero (Figures 4a and 4b). In 2022 July–August, the pooled 59-node Transfer–Scratch contrast was +0.014 K, with a nine-window-adjusted interval of [−0.031, +0.066] K. This near-zero pooled contrast masks opposing bandwise behavior: the contrast was +0.348 K at 0.01–0.1 hPa and negative in the other three bands (Figure 4d). The corresponding observed MLS temperature contrasts are shown separately in Figure 4c. Together, the panels locate a conditional loss of predictive skill within the observed atmospheric context without interpreting it as a causal volcanic effect.

Methods
For each model seed, squared errors and valid counts were pooled over the selected days and pressure nodes before calculating RMSE. Reported model scores are means of the three seed RMSEs. Monthly pressure sections apply the same rule separately to each month and scored node. For the observational W3 context, valid daily MLS means were averaged within each year; the 2020 and 2021 matching-window means were weighted equally, subtracted from 2022, and averaged equally across nodes in each physical pressure band. The annual and Hunga interval families remain separate and retain their frozen definitions.
