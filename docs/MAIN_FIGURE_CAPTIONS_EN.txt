Figure 1. Study region, development diagnostics, and observation use. (a) The evaluation region (11–30°N, 91–129°E); land geometry is derived from Natural Earth 1:50m land, version 4.1.0. (b) SABER minus MLS mean and RMS differences on the common pressure grid. (c) Raw and year-month-demeaned correlations of paired temperatures. Panels (b,c) use 1,028 accepted development pairs in 2020–2021, with 1,010–1,023 valid pairs per node; all 60 development nodes are shown. (d) Actual monthly SABER and MLS profile counts and accepted-pair counts, on separate count axes. (e) Source development and refit, 2020 MLS adaptation budgets, 2021 model selection, and retrospective evaluation in 2022–2025. The reference coordinate z* parameterizes pressure and is not observed geometric altitude.

Figure 2. Target-data efficiency and pressure-band performance. (a) Pooled 2022–2024 RMSE for Transfer, Scratch, ERA5 persistence, source climatology, and the training-only pressure-offset ERA5 reference. The 1%, 10%, and 100% budgets contain 145, 1,446, and 14,463 MLS training profiles. Lines connect the evaluated budgets without fitting a continuous learning curve. (b) Transfer minus Scratch and Transfer minus corrected-ERA5 RMSE, with archived ordinary 95% intervals from 14-day moving-block bootstrap samples. These exploratory intervals are conditional on the three fitted seed pairs and the fitted calibration offsets. (c–e) Paired Transfer and Scratch scores and corrected-ERA5 reference scores in the three fixed reference-node bands, for each budget. Nodes 21–59, 60–70, and 71–79 span approximately 46.78–0.233, 0.203–0.0463, and 0.0396–0.0105 hPa, respectively. Each seed's SSE and valid counts are pooled before calculating RMSE; the three seed RMSEs are then averaged. Negative contrasts favor Transfer.

Figure 3. Monthly pressure structure of the pretraining effect. (a–c) Transfer minus Scratch RMSE at 1%, 10%, and 100% target training budgets, respectively, for every calendar month in 2022–2025. Monthly SSE and valid counts are pooled separately for each fitted seed at each of the 59 scored pressure nodes, and seed RMSEs are then averaged. Cells use logarithmic pressure spacing and are not interpolated or smoothed. All panels share a zero-centered scale of −1 to +1 K; end triangles indicate values beyond these color limits, and complete values are archived in monthly_pressure_all_budgets.csv. Negative values favor Transfer. The dashed time boundary marks January 2025; all displayed years are retrospective evaluations. Aligned profiles at right show the corresponding contrasts after pooling within 2022–2024 (solid) and 2025 (dashed), rather than averaging monthly RMSEs. No significance tests are assigned to individual cells or profile nodes.

Figure 4. Annual and retrospective Hunga-window evidence with aligned observational context. (a) Transfer minus Scratch RMSE in 2022–2024 for three target budgets, with archived 95% Bonferroni familywise intervals across nine year-budget comparisons using 14-day blocks and 10,000 resamples. (b) Corresponding 1%-budget contrasts for nine Hunga windows, with separately adjusted nine-window intervals using 14-day blocks and 100,000 resamples. Filled markers denote an upper adjusted interval bound below zero: four of nine annual comparisons and eight of nine Hunga comparisons. W1 is 15 January–31 March, W2 April–May, and W3 July–August. The open diamond identifies 2022 W3. (c) Observed MLS temperature contrasts for 2022 W3 relative to equally weighted 2020 and 2021 matching-window means, averaged equally across nodes within each physical pressure band. (d) Transfer minus Scratch RMSE for the same window and bands at the 1% budget, pooling SSE and counts within each seed before averaging seed RMSEs. Panels (c,d) have independent axes and show descriptive point estimates. Observed-temperature contrasts and predictive-error contrasts quantify different quantities and do not establish volcanic causation.
