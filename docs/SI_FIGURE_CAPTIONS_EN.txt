Figure S1. Exploratory pressure-band pretraining contrasts. (a–c) Transfer minus Scratch RMSE for 1%, 10%, and 100% budgets, respectively, by evaluation year and fixed reference-node band. Ordinary 95% intervals use 7-day and 14-day moving blocks and are conditional on three fitted seed pairs. (d) Pooled 2022–2024 contrasts for the same bands and budgets. Bands are z* reference-node groups, not geometric-height layers. Negative contrasts favor Transfer; no bandwise familywise claim is made.

Figure S2. Alternative error metrics. Transfer minus Scratch MAE (a–c) and signed bias (d–f) for each budget, evaluated by year and over pooled 2022–2024. The two interval sets are archived ordinary 95% intervals using 7-day and 14-day blocks. Signed error is prediction minus observation; a lower signed bias need not have a smaller absolute value. These are exploratory, fixed-model comparisons.

Figure S3. Development-pair sensitivity on the common pressure grid. Mean SABER minus MLS differences (a), RMS differences (b), and raw Pearson correlations (c) are shown for all pairs, individual years, tighter distance and time limits, and pairs valid at all 60 development nodes. Dotted curves in (a,b) give equal year-month weighting. (d) Monthly mean differences and (e) the fraction of selected pairs valid at each node. Pair counts describe development support and are distinct from model evaluation support.

Figure S4. Training-only ERA5 offset reference and descriptive comparisons. (a) Mean pressure offsets across seeds, with seed minimum–maximum envelopes. (b) Monthly reference RMSE in 2022–2025. (c) Number of months in which Transfer has lower RMSE than each reference, out of 48. (d) Number of the 59 scored nodes at which pooled 2022–2024 Transfer RMSE exceeds corrected-ERA5 RMSE. Counts and seed envelopes are descriptive, not independent significance tests.

Figure S5. Observation coverage and target-label budgets. (a) Monthly evaluated MLS profile counts and yearly totals. (b) MLS product versions consumed in the scoring record and days without observations; the non-existent 366th day is masked for non-leap years. (c) Gradient-training profile counts for 2020 and the 14,417-profile 2021 validation reference used at every budget. The 1% label applies to gradient-training labels; training and validation years are distinct.

Figure S6. Monthly pretraining and corrected-reference contrasts. Transfer minus Scratch RMSE (a–c) and Transfer minus corrected-ERA5 RMSE (d–f) for each budget, displayed by year and month over 2022–2025. All 48 months are retained. Plus signs mark positive point estimates, not significant differences. Each row has a shared symmetric color scale; negative values favor Transfer.

Figure S7. Annual pretraining evidence and exploratory 2025 comparisons. Panels show 1%, 10%, and 100% training budgets. For 2022–2024, intervals are 95% Bonferroni-adjusted intervals across the nine annual year-budget comparisons. For 2025, hollow markers and ordinary 95% intervals denote separate exploratory comparisons. Both 7-day and 14-day block lengths are shown; uncertainty is conditional on the fitted seed pairs. All evaluations are retrospective.

Figure S8. Hunga-window interval definitions at the 1% budget. Transfer minus Scratch RMSE is shown with pointwise 95% intervals (a) and nine-window familywise intervals (b). Both panels use the frozen fixed-seed, 14-day moving-block analysis with 100,000 resamples. W1 is 15 January–31 March, W2 April–May, and W3 July–August. Open markers identify intervals not wholly below zero. These archived intervals are read without rerunning bootstrap.

Figure S9. Exploratory Hunga-window point estimates across budgets and reference-node bands. Transfer minus Scratch RMSE is shown for all 59 scored nodes (a), nodes 21–59 (b), 60–70 (c), and 71–79 (d). Curves connect the three windows in each of 2022, 2023, and 2024 at each training budget. These bands are fixed pressure-reference node groups. No window-by-band significance claim is assigned to the point estimates.

Figure S10. Observed MLS monthly temperature context on the diagnostic native-v06-01 pathway. Monthly 2022–2024 means are compared with equally weighted 2020 and 2021 means for the same calendar month. The vertical coordinate is pressure; the 59 displayed nodes follow the common grid. Contrasts are descriptive and are not volcanic-attribution estimates. The incomplete 2025 native diagnostic record is excluded. This diagnostic pathway is distinct from the mixed-subversion scoring record shown in Figure S5.

Figure S11. Monthly 1%-budget predictive errors in 2022–2024. (a) Transfer, Scratch, and corrected-ERA5 RMSE after pooling monthly SSE and counts within each seed and averaging seed RMSEs. (b) Monthly Transfer minus Scratch RMSE at the 59 scored pressure nodes, now displayed on a logarithmic pressure axis. Negative values favor Transfer. Color cells are descriptive point estimates and are not smoothed or individually significance-tested.

Figure S12. ERA5 monthly context on native L137 levels 1–50. Panels show monthly departures in 2022–2024 from the 2012–2021 matching-calendar-month mean. Model level 1 is at the top. These native model levels are not relabeled as MLS pressure nodes or geometric heights. The ten-year reference is separate from the two-year MLS reference in Figure S10; departures provide atmospheric context without volcanic attribution.
